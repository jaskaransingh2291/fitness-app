                      How it’s worked out

                      For each day you logged food: what you ate minus that day’s maintenance calories. Add those up, and every 7,700 kcal is about 1 kg of body weight. Days with nothing logged are skipped, and today isn’t counted until it’s over.

                      It’s an estimate. Water, salt, and food you forgot to log can make the scale say something different.
